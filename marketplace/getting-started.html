Install

  1. In YouTrack, go to Administration → Apps and install Issue Export from JetBrains Marketplace.
  2. Open the app and make sure it is Active.
  3. Issue Export appears in the YouTrack main menu.

Export issues

  1. Open Issue Export from the main menu.
  2. Choose a project. The other steps become active, and filters and columns are loaded from that project's fields.
  3. Filter the issues (optional):
    • Click + and pick a field, or start typing a field or value name.
    • Tick one or more values in the list that opens. Several values of one field match any of them; different fields are combined.
    • Type any text and press Enter to search for it. Remove a filter with ×, or clear all filters with the × at the right end of the bar.
  4. Choose the columns. Use Add columns to add fields, drag rows (or press Alt+↑/↓) to change their order, and × to remove one.
  5. Check the preview of the first matching issues.
  6. In Export Summary, choose Excel (.xlsx) or CSV (for CSV also the separator) and click Download.

Good to know

  • Exports always contain only issues of the selected project that you are allowed to see.
  • Changing the project resets the filters and columns. Reset starts over.
  • Use the ; separator for CSV files opened in Excel with a Turkish or other European locale, and , for English locales.
  • Your last chosen format and separator are remembered in your browser.
  • Very large exports (thousands of issues) can take a while; a progress bar shows how far it is.
